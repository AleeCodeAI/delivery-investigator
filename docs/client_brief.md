# Delivery Investigator

## Client

**Velora Market** is a mid-sized e-commerce company that manages customer orders and their associated shipment operations.

As order volume grows, Velora Market receives an increasing number of customer complaints related to deliveries. Investigating these complaints requires operations staff to gather information from multiple internal records, understand what happened, identify relevant evidence, and determine how each case should be handled.

As the number of complaints increases, these repetitive investigation tasks consume more staff time and limit how many cases the team can process.

## Problem

Delivery complaints do not always have an obvious explanation. Understanding a case may require examining order and shipment information, reviewing customer history, investigating fulfilment activity, or reconciling inconsistencies across operational records.

Operations employees must spend time retrieving relevant information, connecting related records, reasoning about what the evidence indicates, and documenting their findings before they can make a decision.

This creates an operational bottleneck: **staff spend a significant portion of their time investigating cases instead of focusing on the judgements and decisions that require human attention.**

As complaint volume grows, this can limit the team's investigation capacity and slow down the handling of customer issues.

## Proposed System

**Delivery Investigator** is an AI-powered investigation system designed to help Velora Market process delivery complaints more efficiently.

Its primary objective is to investigate complaints using Velora Market's internal operational records and produce a report explaining the findings. The report identifies the problem the available evidence supports, presents the relevant supporting information, highlights uncertainties or missing evidence, and recommends a potential action based on applicable business policies.

The system is designed to reduce the repetitive work involved in retrieving information, connecting records, and reasoning through operational data. Rather than requiring an employee to conduct every investigation from the beginning, the AI prepares the findings for human review.

## Human Oversight and Decision-Making

Delivery Investigator is intended to **support human employees, not replace them or operate independently of them**.

Multiple complaints can be investigated by the AI concurrently, allowing operations staff to review prepared reports rather than personally perform every retrieval and initial investigation step. Employees can focus their time on assessing the evidence, exercising judgement, and deciding what action should be taken.

The AI may recommend an action according to Velora Market's business policies, but its recommendation is not a final decision. A human operator remains responsible for evaluating the findings, determining whether the recommendation is appropriate, and authorising any consequential action.

The system does not independently issue refunds, create replacement shipments, modify operational records, or communicate resolutions directly to customers.

## Business Impact

Delivery Investigator aims to improve operational efficiency by reducing the time employees spend on repetitive investigation tasks.

Its intended business benefits are to:

- **Reduce investigation time** by automating repetitive information retrieval and initial evidence analysis.
- **Increase investigation capacity** by allowing multiple complaints to be processed concurrently.
- **Improve consistency** by presenting structured findings and supporting evidence across different cases.
- **Support better decisions** by making relevant evidence, uncertainties, and policy-based recommendations available to human operators.
- **Make better use of staff time** by shifting employee effort away from repetitive investigation and towards judgement, decision-making, and complaint resolution.

## Intended Outcome

The goal is to increase the number of delivery complaints that Velora Market can investigate and diagnose within a given period, without sacrificing the quality of the findings or removing human oversight.

The system's success will be measured by how effectively it reduces investigation time, accurately identifies problems supported by the available evidence, provides appropriate policy-based recommendations, and enables operations staff to handle a greater volume of complaints.

**The central objective is to let AI handle more of the repetitive investigation work so that people can focus on the decisions that matter.**
